About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Dartmouth College, where I am fortunate to be advised by Soroush Vosoughi. My research interests lie in natural language processing and machine learning, with a focus on LLM interpretability, reliability, and generation, as well as post-training LLM agents for long-horizon tasks. I'm always looking for related colaboration and internship opportunities. If you are interested to chat with me, feel free to drop me an email.
